# Homepage atmosphere experiments

- **V1 pixel hover:** Home-only Bayer-dithered pixel grid that stays almost white at rest and lights charcoal, sky, amber, and rose cells in a soft radius around the cursor.
- **V2 paper art:** OpenAI-generated high-key ink/watercolor wash as a full-bleed atmosphere, with a paper-white veil for readability and about 12px of mouse parallax.
- **V3 image mosaic:** Generated pastel painting sampled into rounded tiles that wave slowly, lift near the cursor, and stay quieter behind the 800px content column.
- **V4 ink flow (chosen):** Sparse ink filaments on warm paper that draw in slowly along a flow field; dotted construction lines were removed so only the strokes remain.
